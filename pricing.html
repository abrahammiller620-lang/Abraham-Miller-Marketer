Understanding Pricing & Value

Key information regarding quotes, proposals, and project scope.

How are quotes determined?

Pricing is based on campaign scope, manuscript length, genre requirements, timeline urgency, and specific deliverables needed for your book.

When should I reach out for pricing?

Ideally 2 to 4 months prior to your intended launch date. This allows ample time for effective pre-launch positioning and review coordination.

Are ad budgets included in package prices?

Direct advertising spend (such as Amazon or Meta ads) is separate from marketing service fees, ensuring full transparency in your promotional budget.

Can I start with a smaller project and upgrade later?

Yes. Many authors begin with store page optimization or launch preparation, then expand into broader brand building as their release approaches.

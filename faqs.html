01 General Questions

A book marketer creates and executes strategies to help your book get discovered by target readers. This includes optimizing store pages, crafting author brand strategy, planning launch campaigns, preparing press materials, and setting up email funnel strategies to convert casual browsers into long-term fans.

I work with both! Self-published and indie authors often seek comprehensive launch and platform support, while traditionally published authors frequently engage marketing services to supplement publisher efforts and build an independent author brand.

No responsible marketer can guarantee exact sales figures, as market reception depends on many factors (genre trends, cover design, reader reviews, pricing, writing appeal). What I promise is a clear, professional, and strategically sound campaign designed to maximize your book's reach and conversion potential.

02 Services & Strategy

03 Pricing & Timelines

Projects are provided as customized package quotes based on your book's scope, release timeline, and required marketing deliverables. After reviewing your inquiry, I deliver a detailed proposal with clear scope outline.

Campaign timelines typically range from 4 weeks (for targeted platform audits or metadata optimization) to 3–6 months (for full launch campaigns and brand development initiatives).

04 Working Process

All communication is conducted directly via email. You will receive regular project updates, actionable roadmap steps, copy drafts, and feedback responses without needing to manage disruptive meetings.

To begin, simply reach out via the Contact page with your book title, genre, target target publication date, current author website/links (if any), and a brief summary of your marketing goals.
